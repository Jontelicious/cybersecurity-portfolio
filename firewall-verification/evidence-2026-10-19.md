## DNS and internet, 08:30:38–08:30:49

Each guest queried its own .254 gateway for example.com A records using dig over
UDP and TCP. All six replies were NOERROR with A answers, identifying the
expected gateway as server. Results included 104.20.23.154 and 172.66.147.243;
these addresses/TTLs are observations, not fixed test requirements. Cached
responses are still valid evidence of DNS service, not of a fresh upstream query.
Each guest then used curl -4 with proxies bypassed and received HTTP/2 200 from
https://example.com. This demonstrates useful outbound sessions and return traffic.
